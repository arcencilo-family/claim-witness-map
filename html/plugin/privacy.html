CWM plugin: privacy and support

27 October 2026 · Applies to the current skills-only CWM plugin

Publisher: Frank Gerold Boehm, credited in the CWM papers as Frank Böhm · Arcencilo Family Project · Ibiza.

What CWM does

CWM packages instructions and reference papers for guided claim and source assessment inside ChatGPT. It has no CWM-operated server, separate user account, case database, analytics, advertising, or external service connection. The plugin contains no mechanism for sending your case to the publisher or maintaining a publisher-side case archive.

Your conversations and files

Your case text, attachments and generated assessments are processed within the ChatGPT environment to provide the assessment. OpenAI's applicable policies and your account or workspace settings govern that processing, storage, retention and any use for model improvement. CWM does not set those retention periods or override those settings.

Use anonymised cases where possible and share only information you are authorised to provide. Use ChatGPT's available privacy and deletion controls for conversations, files and any saved memories; these can have separate controls and retention rules. This notice does not promise that deleting a chat deletes every separately saved item. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

Support

Use the project's GitHub Issues for bug reports and general help. Do not post case evidence, confidential material, credentials or personal details there. Describe problems using a short, anonymised or synthetic example.

An issue or comment can include your GitHub username, its text and attachments, and activity details supplied by GitHub. This information is public and may be read by the publisher and other visitors. It is used to answer questions, investigate problems and improve CWM. Reports remain as public project history until edited or removed; copies retained by others or by GitHub may follow different rules.

Use GitHub's available editing/removal controls, or ask the project maintainer to review a removal request, identifying the issue without repeating sensitive content. GitHub's processing and retention are governed by its privacy statement. CWM cannot promise deletion from third-party copies or backups.
