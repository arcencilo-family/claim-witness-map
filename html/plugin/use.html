CWM plugin: use and limits

27 October 2026

Publisher: Frank Gerold Boehm, credited in the CWM papers as Frank Böhm · Arcencilo Family Project · Ibiza.

Purpose

Claim × Witness Map helps users locate claims and sources, examine the basis for an assessment, and identify the next useful check. The plugin applies the published CWM v1.0 framework to the material available in a conversation. Its placements are reasoned proposals that users can question and correct.

The plugin does not establish a measured truth probability, authenticate evidence merely by reading it, or turn a matrix label into a legal verdict or other consequential decision. Important findings and any action based on them require appropriate independent checking and human judgment.

Material you provide

Use anonymised examples where possible and provide only material you are authorised to share. Keep confidential evidence and personal details out of public support reports. See the privacy notice for the distinction between ChatGPT processing and public GitHub support.
