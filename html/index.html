Print comfortably

Choose the PDF matching your paper: A4 or US Letter. Print at 100% / Actual size. All three papers use landscape pages; the matrix explanations use three columns; the companion uses two-column landscape pages. All text stays at least 40 points (14.1 mm / 0.56 inch) inside the sheet.

Use the printer's ordinary single-sided or duplex settings. If available for duplex, landscape matrices normally turn on the short edge; check the preview for your printer. PDF margins are checked digitally; physical output depends on the device.
